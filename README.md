# CareerPath

**CareerPath** is a personal career development platform designed to help individuals understand their current skills, explore career opportunities, and build a structured path toward their professional goals.

The platform helps users identify skill gaps between their current capabilities and desired career paths, whether they want to advance in their existing profession, transition into a different career, or explore new opportunities.

### Key Features

- **CV & Skill Analysis** – Upload and analyze resumes to identify existing skills, professional experience, and qualifications.
- **Career Goals** – Define target careers and explore potential career transitions or advancement opportunities.
- **Personalized Learning Paths** – Identify skill gaps and create structured learning plans based on career goals.
- **Learning Progress Tracking** – Monitor learning activities, track skill development, and measure progress toward career objectives.
- **Career & Skill Database** – Maintain a structured database of skills, competencies, and requirements associated with different job roles.
- **External Platform Integration** – Connect with external platforms and services such as LinkedIn, job portals, Udemy, and other learning resources.

### Vision

To become an integrated career development platform that empowers individuals to make data-driven career decisions, continuously improve their skills, and navigate their professional growth with a clear direction.

*CareerPath – Understand Your Skills. Discover Your Direction. Build Your Future.*
